Research Interests

Exotic interactions & dark matter

I study spin-dependent forces mediated by hypothetical bosons, including axions, axion-like particles (ALPs), and paraphotons. I develop theoretical frameworks and interpret constraints from laboratory experiments.

Precision tests & quantum sensing

Atomic and molecular spectroscopy, antimatter experiments, magnetic resonance, levitated magnets, and other precision platforms provide complementary ways to test fundamental symmetries and search for exotic interactions.

Light–matter interaction

Light–matter interactions are a fascinating area of quantum optics. Ultrastrong coupling physics in quantum platforms could enable new approaches to quantum sensing and searches for physics beyond the Standard Model.
