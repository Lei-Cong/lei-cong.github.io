Research Interests

Exotic interactions & dark matter

I study spin-dependent forces mediated by hypothetical bosons, including axions, axion-like particles (ALPs), and paraphotons. I develop theoretical frameworks and interpret constraints from laboratory experiments.

Precision tests & quantum sensing

Atomic and molecular spectroscopy, antimatter experiments, magnetic resonance, levitated magnets, and torsion pendulums provide complementary ways to test fundamental symmetries and search for interactions beyond the Standard Model.

Quantum optics & light–matter interaction

I study light–matter interactions in the ultrastrong coupling regime, focusing on how the quantum Rabi model connects entanglement, quantum phase transitions, and quantum sensing. Trapped ions and other quantum platforms provide ways to explore this physics and may inform the design of new quantum devices for searches beyond the Standard Model.
